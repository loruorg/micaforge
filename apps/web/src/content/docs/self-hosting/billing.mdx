Micaforge can meter what each organisation records, apply plan limits, email the owner as
the quota fills, and sell plans through Stripe. All of it is **off by default**. It exists
for an operator who runs Micaforge as a service for other people. If you run Micaforge for
yourself or your company, leave it off: nothing below applies, nothing is metered against a
quota, and no call is made to Stripe.

With billing off, the **Billing** screen shows the month's usage and says that nothing is
charged, and `MICAFORGE_MAX_SITES_PER_ORG` limits sites as before.

## Turn it on

```sh
MICAFORGE_BILLING_ENABLED=true
MICAFORGE_BILLING_PLANS='[
  {"id":"free","name":"Free","events":10000,"sites":2,"members":3},
  {"id":"team","name":"Team","events":1000000,"sites":20,"members":25,
   "price_id":"price_1Q...","price_label":"$19 a month"}
]'
```

Each plan has:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `id` | Stable id, stored on the organisation. 1 to 32 of `a-z`, `0-9`, `-`, `_`. Never rename one in use. |
| `name` | What the Billing screen calls it. |
| `events` | Monthly event quota. Leave it out, or `null`, for no quota. |
| `sites` | Most sites an organisation on the plan may hold. `null` for no limit. |
| `members` | Most members, pending invitations included. `null` for no limit. |
| `price_id` | The Stripe price that buys the plan. |
| `price_label` | The price as the screen prints it. Stripe is not asked, so the screen works when Stripe is down. |

Exactly one plan must have no `price_id`: it is the plan every organisation is on until it
pays, and the plan it returns to when a subscription ends. The server refuses to start with
a list it cannot use (bad JSON, a repeated id or price, no free plan or two of them) and says
which.

## Limits

With billing on, the organisation's plan sets its limits, and **replaces
`MICAFORGE_MAX_SITES_PER_ORG`**. Adding or moving in a site beyond the plan's `sites` is
refused; instance admins are exempt from that, as they are from
`MICAFORGE_MAX_SITES_PER_ORG`. Inviting a person beyond the plan's `members`, counting
pending invitations, is refused for everybody.

A paid plan applies while its Stripe subscription is `active`, `trialing` or `past_due`
(Stripe's own retry period). Any other status puts the organisation back on the free plan.
Nothing is deleted when that happens: an organisation over the free plan's limits keeps
what it has, and only adding more is refused.

## Usage and the quota

A job meters every organisation once an hour: the rows in `events` (page views, custom
events, errors and Web Vitals) across all its sites in the calendar month, UTC. AI crawler
visits recorded from server logs (`agent_events`) are counted and shown, and never measured
against the quota. The Billing screen also measures on demand when the last count is older
than 15 minutes.

The owner and admins get one email at **80%** of the quota and one at **100%**, once each a
month.

**Nothing is dropped past the quota.** Ingest never reads the meter. An organisation over
its quota keeps recording exactly as before; the events above the quota are recorded as
overage on the month's row, shown on the Billing screen and named in the 100% email.
Overage is not charged automatically. The screen and the emails say so in these words:

> Micaforge keeps recording every event past the quota: nothing is dropped and no tracking
> stops. Events above the quota are counted as overage on the Billing screen, and the owner
> and admins get an email at 80% and at 100%. Choose a larger plan to raise the quota;
> overage is not charged automatically.

## Stripe

```sh
MICAFORGE_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_...
MICAFORGE_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_...
```

Set both or neither; the server refuses to start with one. Micaforge calls Stripe only to
create Checkout sessions and Customer portal sessions.

Without the keys, billing still meters, limits and emails, and every organisation is on the
free plan. To put one on another plan by hand, write its row in the `org_billing` table:

```sql
INSERT INTO org_billing (org_id, plan_id, status) VALUES ('<org id>', 'team', 'active')
ON CONFLICT (org_id) DO UPDATE SET plan_id = excluded.plan_id, status = excluded.status;
```

1. Create a product and a recurring price in Stripe for each paid plan, and put each price
   id in `price_id`.
2. Turn on the **Customer portal** in Stripe's settings, so customers can change their card,
   switch plans and cancel.
3. Add a webhook endpoint at `MICAFORGE_PUBLIC_URL` + `/api/billing/webhook`, for example
   `https://analytics.example.com/api/billing/webhook`, sending these events:

   ```text
   checkout.session.completed
   customer.subscription.created
   customer.subscription.updated
   customer.subscription.deleted
   invoice.payment_failed
   ```

4. Put the endpoint's signing secret in `MICAFORGE_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.

Only the **owner** of an organisation can buy a plan or open the portal. **Choose this plan**
opens Stripe Checkout and comes back to `/app/settings/billing`, which says whether the
payment went through. The plan changes when Stripe's webhook arrives, usually within
seconds.

Every webhook is checked against its `Stripe-Signature` header (HMAC-SHA256 with the
signing secret, and a timestamp no more than five minutes away) before anything in it is
read. An event Stripe sends twice is applied once. A failed payment emails the owner and
shows a warning on the Billing screen.

Plan purchases and subscription changes are in the organisation's
[audit log](/docs/dashboard/audit-log).
